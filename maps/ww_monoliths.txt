ww_monoliths by Zouave
----------------------
Story:
High above the land of Arathas, the rival wizard teams try to 
gain control of a magical floating island in the sky.

Objective: Sequential Territorial Control - Capture all five 
command points in the map in sequential order by running over 
each rune to capture that area.

Scoring: 30 points for capturing all the command points.

Other Notes:

Command Points are color indicated by whichever team controls 
it currently.
A white stream indicates neural status.
Once a point has been captured, it cannot be re-captured for 
15 seconds.  Points have a panel covering them when they 
cannot be re-captured.
