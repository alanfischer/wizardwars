WW_STORM by Zouave

Incremental Capture The Chalice

Objective: Enter the enemy castle and make
your way to where their chalice resides in the
basement. Grab it and bring it back to your
tower. Place it on the raised square to
Capture it. 

Scoring: 
1 point for exiting through the spirals with
the Chalice
1 point for exiting through the castle doors
with the Chalice
8 points per Capture.

Scoring Note:
The exit points are only scored by using the
primary entrances and exits.  Usin shortcuts or 
flying in and out will not score the points.

Other Notes: Chalice carriers drop the Flag when
they die. Dropped chalices return to their Castle
after 60 seconds if not touched by the eneny team.

A Pyromancer can create a reality fissure to open the 
grate above the grailroom to create a shortcut,
it may also be re-sealed by creating a reality fissure
where the grate was.
