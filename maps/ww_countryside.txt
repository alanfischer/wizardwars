================================
 countryside - For Wizard Wars
    - Capture The Grail -
================================
by Final_Commando

Objective: Enter the enemy castle and make
your way to where their Grail resides in 
the chapel. Grab it and bring it back to your
capture point in your respawn room.

Scoring: 10 Points to Capturing Person.
         10 Points to Capturing Team.

Other Notes: Grail carriers drop the grail when
they die. Dropped Grails return to their Base
after 60 seconds of inactivity.

Rideable dragons are available in your base 
to use against the opposition! 
Use them wisely, however, because when a dragon 
dies, it will not respawn again for a few minutes.
