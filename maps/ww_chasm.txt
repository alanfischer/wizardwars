ww_chasm
By: Alan Fischer

On the edge of a mountain range of Arathas, two forces fight for control.
The team of light, blue, must try to gain all of the capture points, and as they do, the clouds will part, and the rain will leave.
But as the dark team, red, gains the points, the storm will become stronger.

The blue team has the help of a yellow dragon rider, as the red has the help of a green dragon rider.

The 5 points to control are:

1.  The Chasm
2.  The Hillside
3.  The Treestump
4.  The Brush
5.  The Hut

Note: The teams must capture the points in order, either 1,2,3,4,5 for blue, or 5,4,3,2,1 for red.
